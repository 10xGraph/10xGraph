# Lumen pricing

## Plans

- **Free**: 1 workspace, 3 dashboards, 10,000 events per month, 7 days of retention.
- **Team**: $29 per seat per month, unlimited dashboards, 5 million events per month,
  90 days of retention.
- **Business**: $99 per seat per month, 50 million events per month, 1 year of retention,
  SSO and audit logs.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the first of each month. Annual billing gives two months free.
Events above your plan limit are billed at $0.40 per additional 100,000 events on Team and
Business. Free workspaces stop collecting events at the limit instead of being billed.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from **Settings > Billing**. Access continues until the end of the
paid period and data is deleted 30 days after that.
