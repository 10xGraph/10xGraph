Lumen security overview

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer and at rest with AES-256.

Single sign-on (SAML 2.0 and OIDC) is available on the Business plan. Audit logs record
sign-ins, dashboard sharing and API key changes, and are kept for one year.

Lumen stores customer data in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia). The region is chosen
when the workspace is created and cannot be changed later.

To report a vulnerability, email security@lumen.example. We acknowledge reports within two
business days.
